## Verification method and boundaries

All nine IDs below returned successful YouTube oEmbed GET responses with exact original title, channel name, channel URL and an embed URL containing the same ID. Metadata was stored during verification and compared exactly against technical.json. Primary manufacturer pages/manuals and original publisher video descriptions were checked with web search/open; where the web reader could not open YouTube directly, indexed original descriptions plus the successful oEmbed response supplied metadata and topic evidence. Selected official HTML pages were also read with GET to confirm exact video IDs.

This verifies identity, provenance and described teaching relevance, not guaranteed future availability, playback on every client, or a complete human viewing of every video. The runtime should retain an external-watch fallback. Original video language is labeled English; the diagrams and accompanying learning guidance are translated into English, Hebrew and Thai. No translated speech, subtitle availability, duration or release date is claimed. No third-party footage was downloaded, edited or rehosted.

All diagrams and questions are original instructional compositions grounded in the owned curriculum and the six legacy lesson topics. They avoid electrical installation schematics or universal field settings. Numerical displays are explicitly identified training examples, not measured performance claims. Product-specific videos illustrate reasoning and observation; current exact-model documentation, qualified supervision and project authority still govern field work.
