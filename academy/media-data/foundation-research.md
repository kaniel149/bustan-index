Foundation arithmetic: 1.2 kW × 5 hours × 30 days = 180 kWh; 8 kWh PV and 8 kWh load with 6 direct creates 2 import and 2 surplus; 10 kWh before reserve × 0.8 × 0.9 = 7.2 kWh AC, divided by 2.4 kW = 3 hours; 14 PV = 11 direct + 3 surplus and 14 load = 11 direct + 3 import. These are training assumptions with explicit boundaries, not system promises or annual forecasts.
